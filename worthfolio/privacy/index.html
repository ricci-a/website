Privacy Policy

WorthFolio · Last updated November 24, 2026

Summary

WorthFolio does not collect, store, share or sell your personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer cannot see your accounts or balances.

Information stored on your device

The accounts, balances, milestones and settings you enter are stored only on your device, inside WorthFolio. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, this data is included in that backup, which is handled by Apple or your computer under your own account. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a short summary (such as your net worth) that the app saves on the same device.

Files you create

Backups, spreadsheet exports and PDF reports are files you choose to create. Where you save or send them (Files, iCloud Drive, email, AirDrop) is your choice and is handled by those services.

Exchange rates (Pro)

If you use more than one currency (an account in another currency, or totals shown in another currency), WorthFolio downloads exchange rates when the app opens (at most once a day), when you choose a new currency, and when you tap Update rates. The request goes to Frankfurter (frankfurter.dev), a free service that publishes European Central Bank reference rates, and contains only currency codes such as CAD and USD. Like any website, the service receives your device's IP address in order to answer. WorthFolio sends no personal or financial information. If your accounts and totals all use one currency, this request is never made.

Purchases

WorthFolio Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment under Apple's own Privacy Policy. WorthFolio only learns whether Pro is unlocked; it never sees your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Face ID and reminders

Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode is checked by your device; WorthFolio only learns whether unlocking succeeded. Balance reminders are notifications scheduled on your device.

Emailing the developer

WorthFolio never asks for your email address, and you never need one to use it. The developer only hears from you if you choose to send an email yourself, for example with Report a problem. Then the developer receives what you send: your email address, your message, and the app version, iOS version and device model included in the draft (you can edit or remove them before sending). This is used only to reply and to fix problems, and is never shared or sold. You can ask for these messages to be deleted at any time.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with the developer. These don't identify you. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

Because your data is only on your device, you are in control of it. Delete accounts in the app, or delete WorthFolio to remove everything it stored. Backup files you saved elsewhere must be deleted there.

Children

WorthFolio is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page and in the app with a new date.

Questions and answers

How is net worth worked out?

Everything you own minus everything you owe. Each account uses its latest balance on or before that day.

What if I don't update an account?

Its last balance carries on until you enter a new one. Nothing is guessed in between.

Can I add past balances?

Yes. Pick a date on the keypad, or add earlier balances when you create an account.

Can I enter a future date?

Yes. It only counts from that day on.

How is "this year" worked out?

Today's net worth minus your net worth on December 31 (or your first balance, if you started later).

How is the monthly amount for a milestone found?

What's left to reach it, divided by the months left. Pro also shows your real pace and whether you're on track.

How do I enter debts?

As a positive number on a debt account. Paying it down shows in green because your net worth goes up.

What does archiving do?

It hides the account and sets it to $0 from today. Your past net worth doesn't change. Bring it back any time.

How do other currencies work? (Pro)

Each account can have its own currency. Totals are converted with exchange rates that update when the app opens, or that you type in.

How does statement import work? (Pro)

On an account, choose the CSV file from your bank. WorthFolio finds each day's closing balance and shows a preview first.

Where is my data?

Only on this device. WorthFolio saves a recovery copy every day. To move to a new phone, use Back up now and restore the file there.

Restoring Pro

Pro is tied to your Apple Account. On a new device, open Settings → Go Pro → Restore purchase.

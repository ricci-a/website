WorthFolio

A simple, private net worth tracker. Open the app, see your whole financial picture, update your balances. Done.

Coming soon to the App StoreSupport

No bank logins. Ever.

WorthFolio never asks for your bank password. You type your balances or import a statement you downloaded. There's no account, no ads and no tracking, and everything stays on your device.

What it does

One number

Everything you own minus everything you owe, with a chart you can scrub to any day.

Private by design

No bank logins, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your device and works offline.

Update in seconds

Type a balance and save, or import a bank statement file.

See where it lives

Breakdowns by type, monthly changes, your best and toughest months.

Set milestones

Pick a target and a date. See what it takes each month and when you'll get there.

Widgets

Your net worth on the Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Free, with an optional Pro

Tracking, history, milestones and backups are free. Pro is a one-time purchase, with no subscription, that adds every chart range, Trends, milestone planning, more currencies, import, export and reports.
